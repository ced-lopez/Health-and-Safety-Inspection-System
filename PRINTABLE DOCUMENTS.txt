
PRINTABLE DOCUMENTS

====================================================
DOCUMENT GENERATION & PRINTING
====================================================

The system must support generating professional printable documents in PDF format suitable for government use.

All generated documents should have consistent formatting and include:

- Barangay 178 Official Header
- Barangay Logo
- Republic of the Philippines Header
- North Caloocan City
- Document Title
- Unique Document Number
- Date Generated
- QR Code (where applicable)
- Authorized Signatory
- Signature Line
- Official Seal Placeholder
- Footer with system-generated disclaimer

All printable documents must be downloadable and printable in A4 paper size.

====================================================
PRINTING REQUIREMENTS
====================================================

All printable documents should:

- Be generated as PDF.
- Be printable directly from the web application.
- Maintain professional government formatting.
- Support preview before printing.
- Automatically paginate long tables.
- Include page numbers.
- Include the generation timestamp.
- Include the user who generated the document.
- Preserve formatting across browsers.
- Support reprinting at any time.

====================================================
PDF LIBRARY
====================================================

Use a reliable PDF generation library compatible with the project's technology stack.

The generated PDFs should maintain a consistent layout, support tables, images, QR codes, and official branding.
