## 2. NEW BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS

### Inspection Workflow

- There is **no fixed inspection schedule**.
- Residents submit inspection requests.
- Barangay Staff reviews the request and assigns an available inspector.
- Supported inspection categories:
  - Food Establishment (e.g., Carinderia, Restaurant, Canteen, Bakery, Water Refilling Station, Grocery, Convenience Store handling prepared food, Food Stall/Kiosk)
  - Piggery
  - Poultry
  - Dog Raising / Kennel

### Resident Registration

Residents must register before requesting inspections. Resident information includes:

- Full Name
- Age
- Address
- Contact Number
- Email

### Application Requirements (Category-Specific)

#### General Requirements (All Applicants)

Every applicant must provide:

- Barangay Account (registered account in the system)
- Valid Government-issued ID
- Complete Applicant Information
- Contact Number
- Email Address
- Business or Facility Address
- Type of Establishment/Facility
- Purpose of Application (New Clearance / Renewal)
- Uploaded Supporting Documents

#### Category: Food Establishment

**Examples:** Carinderia, Restaurant, Canteen, Bakery, Water Refilling Station, Grocery, Convenience Store (if handling prepared food), Food Stall or Kiosk

**Possible Requirements:**
- Valid Government-issued ID
- Business Permit (if existing)
- DTI/SEC Registration (if applicable)
- Mayor's Permit (for renewal, if applicable)
- Sanitary Permit (if required by the barangay)
- Sketch or Business Location
- Previous Health and Safety Clearance (renewal only)

**Inspection Focus:**
- Food sanitation and hygiene
- Cleanliness of the establishment
- Proper food handling and storage
- Water supply and sanitation
- Waste disposal and segregation
- Pest control measures
- Kitchen and food preparation area sanitation
- Ventilation and workplace safety
- Employee hygiene practices (where applicable)

#### Category: Piggery

**Possible Requirements:**
- Valid ID
- Proof of Ownership or Occupancy
- Business Registration (if commercial)
- Sketch of Location
- Previous Clearance (renewal)

**Inspection Focus:**
- Animal housing
- Drainage
- Waste management
- Odor control
- Distance from residential areas
- Environmental sanitation

#### Category: Poultry

**Possible Requirements:**
- Valid ID
- Proof of Ownership
- Business Registration (if commercial)
- Sketch of Facility
- Previous Clearance (renewal)

**Inspection Focus:**
- Cage cleanliness
- Waste disposal
- Water source
- Biosecurity measures
- Animal health conditions

#### Category: Dog Raising / Kennel

**Possible Requirements:**
- Valid ID
- Proof of Ownership
- Vaccination Records (if applicable)
- Previous Clearance (renewal)

**Inspection Focus:**
- Kennel sanitation
- Animal welfare
- Vaccination compliance
- Waste disposal
- Noise and nuisance control

> **Note:** "Food Establishment" expands the original "Business" category with concrete examples and its own document/inspection rules. The Compliance Checklist Module (Section 4.5) and Document Upload Module (Section 5.4) must be built to support **per-category dynamic requirement sets and checklists**, not a single fixed list — since required documents and inspection focus areas differ by category and application type (new vs. renewal).

### Inspector Mobile Workflow

- Inspector uses a Flutter mobile app connected to the same API.
- The mobile app must support **offline inspection** with **automatic synchronization** once connectivity resumes.

### Violation & Compliance Flow

1. If violations are found → generate a **Violation Notice**.
2. Applicant has **7 days** to comply.
3. Applicant requests a **follow-up inspection**.
4. Inspector performs the follow-up inspection.
5. If compliant → generate a **QR-coded Health and Safety Clearance**, valid for **1 year**.

### QR Verification

The QR code verifies **only the final clearance** and opens a public verification page displaying:

- Verification Status
- Clearance Number
- Applicant Name
- Business Name (if applicable)
- Inspection Category
- Issue Date
- Expiration Date
- Issuing Authority

### Required Email Notifications

- Registration
- Application Submitted
- Missing Requirements
- Inspector Assigned
- Inspection Completed
- Violation Notice
- Follow-up Inspection
- Clearance Approved
- Renewal Reminders
- Clearance Expiration
